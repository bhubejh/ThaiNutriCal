OUR LITTLE PROJECT 🇹🇭

Making Thai food
easier to understand.

ThaiNutri is my school project. I wanted to make a small website that makes nutrition information easier to understand.

🍚Good food.
Good questions.Learn what is on your plate.
01

Why Thai food?

Thai meals are incredibly varied. A school canteen, local market, food stall and family kitchen can all have completely different meals. We wanted our project to feel connected to the food people actually see around them.

02

What does ThaiNutri do?

You choose a food and enter its amount in grams. The calculator then estimates calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat for that portion. Recipes give you familiar examples to explore.

03

Built for learning

The goal isn't to make food stressful. It is to help students become curious about what different foods contain and learn how nutrition numbers work.
